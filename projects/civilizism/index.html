🏠 Civilizism

What if you simulated one person really well, then simulated a lot of them?

如果把一個人模擬得夠像，再把一大群模擬代理人放在一起，會怎麼樣？

1The problem問題

Most social simulations hardcode the outcome: the rules say how groups behave, and, surprise, they behave that way. I wanted the opposite. Build one believable individual, then see whether groups, beliefs and norms show up on their own.

大多數社會模擬都先把規則鎖死，然後……非常神奇地，群眾真的照著規則行動了。所以我想反過來：先打造一個可信的個體，再複製出一大堆，讓他們自己互動，看看群體、信念和規範會不會自己長出來。

2My solution我的解法

Agents that perceive, remember, reflect and plan, with memory as the hub of everything. Whatever beliefs and relationships form are a side effect and the surprise my product gives me in return. The model only proposes actions; a deterministic utility engine called SHAPE scores each one against 43 personality coefficients (Schwartz values plus HEXACO traits) and decides which actually happens. Character is enforced by code, not by prompting. AI is a powerful wild horse, but it's only a good ride if you put a mathematical rein on it.

這些代理人會感知、記憶、反思、規劃，而記憶是整個系統的核心。至於會長出什麼信念和關係，完全是副產品，也是這個產品順便送我的驚喜。模型只負責提議要做什麼；真正決定哪件事會發生的，是一個叫SHAPE的確定性效用引擎，它會根據43個人格係數（Schwartz價值觀加HEXACO人格特質），替每個提議打分數。角色的個性是靠程式碼硬性規定的，不是靠prompt去拜託。AI再強，不受控也是白搭。

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

LLM personalities drift based on vibes. In my earliest experiments, the four simulated housemates spent all day either snacking or on their way to snack, looping between the kitchen and the living room, and once tried to blend smoothies from ingredients that didn't exist. (Facts aside... that lifestyle is honestly tempting.) Then they started hiding their sadness behind cheerful small talk, and I had to ask whether that was a bug or a personality trait. Humans do it constantly, so I refused to hardcode a fix and put the control in code instead.

LLM的人格很容易憑感覺越飄越遠。我最早的實驗中那四個模擬室友整天不是在吃零食，就是在去吃零食的路上，一直在廚房和客廳之間鬼打牆，有一次還想用根本不存在的食材打果昔。（撇開事實不談……這生活確實讓人很心動。）後來他們開始用輕鬆的閒聊，掩飾自己的難過，我只好想：這到底是bug，還是個性？人本來就會這樣，所以我不想硬寫一條規則去修掉它，而是把控制放進程式碼裡。

The validation story is the nerdy one: I'm checking the whole thing against Milgram, Asch, Robbers Cave and the Stanford Prison Experiment, with predictions written down before any run so I can't cheat. The Stanford case is built so that a null result agrees with the modern reading of that study. The harness is done; the scored runs are still pending...waiting on the day I feel like letting my laptop run through an entire night and hopefully not burning down while I sleep through it.

驗證的部分是最宅的：我拿整個系統去對照Milgram、Asch、Robbers Cave和史丹佛監獄實驗，而且預測都是在跑之前就先寫好的，所以我沒辦法作弊。史丹佛那組我設計成，就算結果是什麼都沒發生，也符合現在學界對那個實驗的看法。測試框架已經做完了，正式計分的跑次還在等……等哪天我願意讓筆電整晚開著跑，而且祈禱它不要在我睡著的時候燒起來。

4Tech stack & what I learned技術堆疊與我學到的

PythonNumPysentence-transformersLiteLLM + InstructorPydanticReact

Pydantic saved my life when parsing LLM output with regex was a living hell. This was also my first real step into vibe coding. And modelling humans, drawing on psychology and biology and turning it all into functions and memories, was a living hell of its own kind.

用regex解析LLM的輸出簡直是地獄，是Pydantic救了我。這也是我第一次真正踏進vibe coding。而要模擬人類——從心理學、生物學下手，再全部變成函式和記憶——本身又是另一種地獄。
