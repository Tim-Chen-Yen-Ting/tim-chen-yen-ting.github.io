💬 Past Messages Analyzer

What if you could watch a friendship's tone drift over time?

如果你能看見一段友情的語氣如何隨時間漂移呢？

1The problem問題

Years of messages with the same people, and no real sense of how those relationships changed. Weirdly emotional for a stats project.

和同一群人傳了好幾年的訊息，卻完全不知道這些關係是怎麼變的。對統計專題來說，情緒過於豐富。

2My solution我的解法

A pipeline that ingests Facebook Messenger and Instagram exports into a normalized SQLite database, then layers analysis on top: response latency, sentiment, and rolling drift in vocabulary and tone, with changepoint detection to flag when a relationship's style actually shifts. It reuses BLOC's incremental clustering to track relationship drift in embedding space.

一條把 Facebook Messenger 和 Instagram 匯出資料灌進正規化 SQLite 資料庫的流程，再往上疊加分析：回覆延遲、情緒，以及詞彙與語氣的滾動漂移，並用變點偵測標出一段關係的風格何時真的轉變。它重用了 BLOC 的增量分群，在嵌入空間裡追蹤關係漂移。

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

The real design question was privacy. They're my friends' messages, so the AI narration layer only sees summary statistics by default. A close-reading mode exists for statistically flagged anomalies, but it's opt-in and off by default, a deliberate trade of interpretability for privacy.

真正的設計問題是隱私。這些是我朋友的訊息，所以 AI 的敘述層預設只看得到摘要統計。針對統計上被標記的異常有精讀模式，但它需要主動開啟、預設關閉，這是刻意拿可解釋性換隱私。

4Tech stack & what I learned技術堆疊與我學到的

PythonSQLitepandasVADERSnowNLPPELT changepointsJupyter

Unsurprisingly, you need two sentiment tools when your friends text in two languages. Also, welcome to the world of psychology: my stats skills get challenged by sentiment, at least according to the psych textbooks.

毫不意外，當你的朋友用兩種語言傳訊息，你就需要兩套情緒分析工具。另外，歡迎來到心理學的世界：至少根據心理學教科書，我的統計能力正被情緒挑戰著。
