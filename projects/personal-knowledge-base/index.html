📚 Personal Knowledge Base

What if your notes could tell you what you're missing?

如果你的筆記，會告訴你還缺什麼呢？

1The problem問題

What if you have a pile of documents, no way to ask questions across them, and no idea what you haven't covered yet? Welcome to Personal Knowledge Base, built for a non-technical user.

當你有一大堆文件，卻沒辦法跨文件提問，也不知道自己還有哪些東西沒涵蓋到的話，怎麼辦？歡迎來到Personal Knowledge Base，這是專門為非技術背景的使用者做的。

2My solution我的解法

Drop in a PDF, Word doc or text file. An LLM turns it into a tagged wiki entry, you can ask questions against the whole knowledge base (or just some topics) with sources cited, and a per-topic "knowledge audit" lists your strengths, gaps and what to read next. Nothing humbles you like a list of what you haven't read. Yes, the front end is ugly. It works.

把PDF、Word或文字檔丟進去，LLM會把它變成一篇有標籤的wiki條目；你可以對整個知識庫（或只針對幾個主題）提問，答案還會附上出處；每個主題的「知識審計」會列出你的強項、缺口，還有接下來該讀什麼。沒有什麼比一份「你還沒讀的東西」清單，更能讓人謙虛。對，前端很醜。但它能用。

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

Left alone, LLM tagging invents near-duplicates ("ml", "machine-learning", "machine learning") until the tags are useless, so each ingest shows the model the existing tags and tells it to reuse them. I also chose to answer questions by loading the relevant entries into the prompt instead of building a vector search. At this scale it's simpler, cheaper and easier to debug, and the sources are exactly what the model saw.

放著不管的話，LLM打標籤會自己發明一堆長得差不多的標籤（「ml」、「machine-learning」、「machine learning」），最後標籤就全都沒用了，所以每次匯入，都會先讓模型看過現有的標籤，並要求它重複使用。我也選擇直接把相關條目放進prompt來回答問題，而不是去做向量搜尋。在這個規模下，這樣更簡單、更便宜、更容易除錯，而且出處就剛好是模型實際看到的內容。
