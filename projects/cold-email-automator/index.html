📬 Cold Email Automator

What if the job hunt did its own legwork?

如果求職的跑腿工作，能自己跑完呢？

1The problem問題

Can't find a job? Try cold emailing. I'm told it works, if you don't count the middle: figuring out who to contact, finding a working address, and writing something that isn't obviously a template.

當你實在找不到工作時，不如試試看陌生開發信吧。聽說那是有用的，如果不算過程的話……要想該聯絡誰、找到能用的信箱，還要寫得不像一眼就看得出來是模板。

2My solution我的解法

A pipeline I use on my own job search. Claude researches targets with web search, an email verifier checks the addresses, a draft is written around one specific hook (a recent launch, a piece of news), and the email goes out through Gmail while a Google Sheets dashboard tracks status, bounces and replies. Yes, I automated the job hunt; someone still has to read the replies.

這是我自己求職時在用的流程。Claude用網路搜尋去研究目標，信箱驗證器檢查地址有沒有效，然後圍繞一個具體的切入點（最近的新產品、一則新聞）寫信，再透過Gmail寄出，同時用Google Sheets儀表板追蹤狀態、退信和回覆。對，我把求職自動化了；不過回信，還是得有人來讀。

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

Keeping it from finding the same few companies forever and carpet-bombing the same person, so each run is told to skip the 100 most recent. Then there's cost: roughly a few cents per sent email depending on the provider. I also made the LLM layer provider-agnostic, so Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini are interchangeable from a config file. Keep an eye on API rates, which lately move about as much as the stock market.

要避免它永遠只找到同樣的幾間公司，然後一個勁地朝著同一個人信件轟炸，所以每次執行時都需要讓LLM知道要跳過最近的100間。但別忘了成本的問題：依供應商不同，每封寄出去的信大約幾美分。我也把LLM那一層做成不綁特定供應商，所以Claude、GPT-4o和Gemini，改一下設定檔就可以互換。一起盯著最新的API rate吧，最近API rate跟股市差不多上上下下的。

4Tech stack & what I learned技術堆疊與我學到的

PythonPydanticSQLiteGmail APIGoogle Sheets APIClaude / GPT-4o / Gemini

I learned OAuth the hard way: Gmail's credentials are not bypassable when you mass-send from scripts, no matter how clever you feel. Also, my wallet is crying from the API cost.

我是吃了苦頭才學會OAuth：用腳本大量寄信的時候，Gmail的憑證機制是繞不過去的，不管你覺得自己有多聰明。還有，我的錢包正在為了API費用哭泣。

Code原始碼

功能都做完了，也測過了。我故意先不部署，等求職的時機對了再說。
