📬 Cold Email Automator

What if the job hunt did its own legwork?

如果求職的跑腿工作能自己做完呢？

1The problem問題

Cold outreach works, but the middle is miserable: figuring out who to contact, finding a working address, and writing something that isn't obviously a template.

陌生開發信有效，但中間的過程很痛苦：搞清楚該聯絡誰、找到能用的信箱，還要寫出一封看起來不像模板的信。

2My solution我的解法

A pipeline I use on my own job search. Claude researches targets with web search, an email verifier checks the addresses, a draft is written around one specific hook (a recent launch, a piece of news), and the email goes out through Gmail while a Google Sheets dashboard tracks status, bounces and replies. Yes, I automated the job hunt; someone still has to read the replies.

我自己求職時用的流程。Claude 用網路搜尋研究目標，信箱驗證器檢查地址，信件圍繞一個具體的切入點（最近的產品發布、一則新聞）撰寫，再透過 Gmail 寄出，同時由 Google Sheets 儀表板追蹤狀態、退信與回覆。對，我把求職自動化了；回信還是得有人讀。

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

Keeping it from researching the same companies forever (each run is told to skip the 100 most recent), and keeping it cheap: roughly a few cents per sent email depending on the provider. I also made the LLM layer provider-agnostic, so Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini are interchangeable from a config file.

不讓它永遠重複研究同樣的公司（每次執行都會被告知跳過最近的 100 間），還有控制成本：依供應商不同，每封寄出的信大約幾美分。我也把 LLM 層做成與供應商無關，所以 Claude、GPT-4o 和 Gemini 只要改設定檔就能互換。

4Tech stack & what I learned技術堆疊與我學到的

PythonPydanticSQLiteGmail APIGoogle Sheets APIClaude / GPT-4o / Gemini

I learned OAuth the hard way: Gmail's credentials are not bypassable when you mass-send from scripts, no matter how clever you feel. Also, my wallet is crying from the API cost.

我用最痛的方式學會了 OAuth：當你用腳本大量寄信時，Gmail 的憑證機制是繞不過去的，不管你覺得自己多聰明。另外，我的錢包正在為 API 費用哭泣。

5Code原始碼

Code原始碼

Feature-complete and tested. I'm deliberately holding off deploying it until the timing of my job search makes sense.

功能完整且經過測試。我刻意先不部署，等到求職的時機合適再說。
