⏰ WhenWhere

What if you need to find some time to meet a friend on the other side of the globe?

如果你要跟地球另一邊的朋友喬時間，怎麼辦？

1The problem問題

As an international student from Taiwan, doing LA-to-Taipei math in my head is daily life. It's a worse problem than it sounds: only one of the two places does daylight saving, so the offset changes twice a year, and one flight can take you from +16 to -16. And I'm lazy.

身為一個從台灣來的國際學生，在腦中算洛杉磯和台北的時差是我的日常生活。但兩邊只有一邊有日光節約時間，所以時差一年會變兩次，而且搭一次飛機就要從+16變成-16，這件事比聽起來更麻煩。而且別忘了我很懶：）

2My solution我的解法

Add cities to a day/night map, say when each person is free, and it shows when everyone overlaps. Every other city on the map turns green or red depending on whether it could still join. There's also a simple point-in-time converter. Daylight saving and half-hour timezones are handled properly, so you can trust this more than the old fashioned clock on your wall...as long as you can find your city on the map.

在日夜地圖上加入城市，說出每個人什麼時候有空，它就會告訴你大家的空檔在哪裡重疊。地圖上其他城市，則會依照「還來不來得及加入」變成綠色或紅色。另外也有單純的時間點換算。日光節約時間和半小時的時區，它也能處理，所以比起牆上那個老派的時鐘，你更可以相信它…如果你找得到自己的城市在哪的話

3Challenges & fun stories挑戰與趣事

For cities far enough apart, the real overlap can land on adjacent calendar days, so we can call each other tomorrow, but your tomorrow may not be my tomorrow. "Same local window" has to be treated as a recurring daily pattern, not the date you typed. And the map itself fought me: Russia, Fiji and Antarctica drew spurious bands across the whole world because their polygons cross the antimeridian and the map library doesn't wrap. I had to split the rings.

如果兩個城市離得夠遠，真正重疊的時段，可能會落在相鄰的兩天而不是同一個日曆日，所以我們可以明天打電話，但你的明天可能不是我的明天。地圖本身也跟我作對：俄羅斯、斐濟和南極洲的多邊形會跨過國際換日線，但地圖函式庫不會自動接起來，結果整個世界被畫上一堆莫名其妙的條紋。我只好把那些多邊形的邊界切開。

A dataset I found online saved me once, but some cities always slip through, so if yours isn't on the map, custom cities are another puzzle. Their timezone is inferred from the nearest known city, longitude first, since timezones are basically longitude bands, with a distance cutoff so a far-away neighbor's political quirks (looking at you, Adelaide) don't come along.

雖然網路上的資料庫已經救過我一次了，但總有漏網之魚的城市嘛，所以如果你找不到你的所在地……自訂城市又成了另一個難題。它的時區，是從最近的已知城市推出來的，先看經度，因為時區基本上就是一條一條的經度帶，再加上距離上限，免得被遠方鄰居的政治怪癖影響（說的就是你，阿德雷德）。

4Tech stack & what I learned技術堆疊與我學到的

PythonFastAPIpytzLeafletIntl APIpytestRender

Civilizism taught me how to build my own framework. WhenWhere taught me when not to, because that has another name: reinventing the wheel. Thank god pytz exists, and there are already datasets online. The timezone math also runs in the browser, mirroring a tested backend, so most interactions need no round trip.

Civilizism教我怎麼自己蓋框架，WhenWhere教我什麼時候不要自己蓋，因為那有另一個名字：閉門造車。感謝老天有pytz，網路上也早就有現成的資料集。時區的計算也會在瀏覽器裡跑，對照一個測試過的後端，所以大部分操作都不用跟伺服器來回。
